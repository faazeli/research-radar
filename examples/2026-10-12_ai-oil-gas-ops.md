## 1. [Physics-Based Decline Curve Analysis and Machine Learning for Temperature Forecasting in Enhanced Geothermal Systems: Utah FORGE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03283)
Mina S. Khalaf · arXiv physics.soc-ph · 2026-01-02 · **relevance 8/10** · `decline curve analysis` `temperature forecasting` `Enhanced Geothermal Systems`

**TL;DR:** Researchers developed a physics-based framework for decline-curve analysis and temperature forecasting in Enhanced Geothermal Systems, achieving near-perfect fidelity and strong predictive accuracy.

- Median RMSE = 0.071 °C across the simulation dataset
- MAE = 3.06 °C and RMSE = 4.49 °C for the equation-informed network
- RMSE = 3.39 °C; MAE = 2.34 °C for the Gaussian Process surrogate

**Why it matters:** This study's findings matter for the development of reliable temperature forecasting models in Enhanced Geothermal Systems, which is essential for efficient and safe operation.

- 87.6% production forecasting precision
- 91.4% well placement optimization success rate

**Why it matters:** This research demonstrates the practical integration of cutting-edge AI technologies with petroleum domain expertise for enhanced operational efficiency, safety, and economic performance in upstream production.

## 3. [Data-driven models for production forecasting and decision supporting in petroleum reservoirs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18289)

**TL;DR:** Researchers developed a data-driven approach using machine learning methods to forecast production parameters in petroleum reservoirs without relying on geological models or fluid properties.

- Applied methodology to real plays in the Brazilian pre-salt

**Why it matters:** This research aims to design a reliable predictor for reproducing reservoir dynamics, supporting reservoir management and maximizing oil recovery.
